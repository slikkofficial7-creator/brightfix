About BrightFix Electrical Services

BrightFix Electrical Services is a fictional small business created for this website project. It represents an electrical service provider focused on dependable work, customer safety and professional service.

Our History

BrightFix is described in this assignment as having been established in 2021 to support residential and small commercial customers. The fictional organisation aims to build customer relationships through clear communication and quality workmanship.

Our Mission

To provide safe, reliable and professional electrical services while delivering quality workmanship and helpful customer service.

Our Vision

To become a trusted electrical services provider in Gauteng, recognised for professional workmanship, dependable service and a commitment to customer safety.

Our Values

Safety

We place customer and worker safety first.

Reliability

We aim to provide dependable electrical support.

Quality

We value careful workmanship and clear communication.
