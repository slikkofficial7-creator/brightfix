About BrightFix Electrical Services

BrightFix Electrical Services is a professional electrical services business established in 2021.

We provide reliable electrical solutions for residential and small commercial customers, with a focus on quality, safety and customer satisfaction.

Our History

BrightFix Electrical Services was established in 2021 as a small electrical services business. The organisation was created to provide customers with reliable, professional and safety-focused electrical solutions.

Since its establishment, BrightFix has aimed to build strong relationships with customers by providing dependable electrical services and professional workmanship.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide safe, reliable and professional electrical services while delivering quality workmanship and excellent customer service.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted electrical services provider in Gauteng, recognised for professional workmanship, reliable service and our commitment to customer safety.

Our Values

  • Safety: We place customer and employee safety first.
  • Reliability: We aim to provide dependable electrical solutions.
  • Quality: We are committed to quality workmanship.
